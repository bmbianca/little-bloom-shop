## Progress update - first interactions
Everything below is in `ver2_scene` and can be played from start to finish.

**Shop and movement (Irina)**
- XR Origin with controllers, teleport on the floor and snap turn
- Front wall with the door and two shop windows
- Open / Closed sign and the score board on the wall

**Flowers and bouquet (Andreea)**
- Flowers that can be picked up from the vases one by one
- Flowers dropped on the paper join the bouquet
- The finished bouquet can be lifted as one object

**Cutting (Bianca)**
- Scissors you can pick up and close with the trigger
- Stems, wrapping paper and ribbons can be cut
- Cut pieces can be thrown in the trash bin, where they disappear

**Wrapping (Olivia)**
- Wall with paper rolls and ribbon spools
- Pull paper from a roll and place it on the table
- Fold the two sides of the paper over the flowers to make the cone
- Bring a ribbon to the base of the bouquet to tie a bow
- White outlines show where to place the paper, the flowers and the ribbon

**How to test**
1. Open `Assets/Scenes/ver2_scene`.
2. With keyboard and mouse: choose *Little Bloom > Grab Mode > Toggle*, then press Play.
3. With a headset: disable *XR Device Simulator*, choose *Little Bloom > Grab Mode > Hold*, then press Play.
